# A short session showing resting orders, time priority, partial fills,
# walking the book, IOC, cancels and reduces.

# Build up both sides of the book
LIMIT 1 S 101.00 300
LIMIT 2 S 100.50 200
LIMIT 3 S 100.50 100     # same price as #2, queued behind it
LIMIT 4 B 99.75  400
LIMIT 5 B 100.00 250
PRINT

# Aggressive buy crosses the spread: fills all of #2 first (time
# priority), then 50 of #3 at the same price
LIMIT 6 B 100.50 250
PRINT

# Market sell walks down the bids
MARKET 7 S 400

# IOC buy takes what it can at <= 101.00 and cancels the rest
IOC 8 B 101.00 500
PRINT

# Manage resting orders
LIMIT 9  B 99.90 300
LIMIT 10 S 100.80 150
REDUCE 9 100
CANCEL 4
CANCEL 42                # unknown id
PRINT
